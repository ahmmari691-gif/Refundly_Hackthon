Terms & privacy.

What this demo provides

Informational deposit reviews and editable document drafts. No attorney-client relationship is formed and no result is guaranteed. Review applicable law and your specific facts before relying on a draft.

Payments and refunds

Checkout is simulated. The renter checkout does not collect card details or email addresses and does not use Stripe. No money is charged, so there is no real payment to refund. Manager refund screens operate on clearly labelled synthetic records only. No money-back guarantee is offered by this demo.

Your draft

When you request a review or generate a document, your entries are transmitted to the Refundly Python server hosted on Render for calculation and document preparation. The application does not save renter cases or generated documents on the server; documents are generated in memory and returned to your browser. A draft is saved in this browser tab using session storage. Use “Clear my browser draft” to remove that browser copy. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

Staff data

Real staff accounts (including staff email addresses), sessions, configuration, synthetic support cases, and audit logs are saved in a SQLite database on the hosted server. Real staff access requires a password and authenticator code. Demo admin and Demo manager use synthetic data in the browser and do not grant real staff access. The free hosted demo uses ephemeral storage, so server-side staff data and edits may reset after a restart or redeployment.

External resources

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Official legal source links open external sites. Form content is not included in those requests. No advertising or analytics trackers are configured.

Support

No public support mailbox is configured yet. Contact the project team. Do not enter real personal data into the synthetic staff support queue.
