Terms & privacy.

What this demo provides

Informational deposit reviews and editable document drafts. No attorney-client relationship is formed and no result is guaranteed. Review applicable law and your specific facts before relying on a draft.

Payments and refunds

Checkout is simulated. No cards, Stripe payments, or email addresses are collected. No money is charged, so there is no real payment to refund. Manager refund screens operate on clearly labelled synthetic records only. No money-back guarantee is offered by this demo.

Your draft

Renter input is sent to the Python server running on your computer for calculation and document preparation, without being stored as a case. A draft is saved in this browser tab using session storage. Use “Clear my browser draft” to remove it. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

Staff data

Staff accounts, sessions, configuration, synthetic support cases, and audit logs are saved in the local SQLite database. Staff access requires a password and authenticator code. This local demonstration is not a production data-security or compliance guarantee.

External resources

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Official legal source links open external sites. Form content is not included in those requests. No advertising or analytics trackers are configured.

Support

No public support mailbox is configured yet. Contact the person running this local demonstration. Do not enter real personal data into the synthetic staff support queue.
