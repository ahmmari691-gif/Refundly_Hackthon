ONE STEP AT A TIME

Keep your next move simple.

1. Save your evidence

Keep your lease, payment records, itemization, photos, envelopes, and proof of your forwarding address.

2. Send your reviewed letter

Check the facts and delivery requirements. Keep proof of delivery. Florida generally gives you 15 days from receipt of a claim notice to object.

3. Set a follow-up

Choose your own follow-up date. This reminder is not a legal filing deadline.

4. Consider small claims

Check your state court’s official website for venue, claim limits, filing deadlines, service requirements, and fees. Prepare a timeline and consider local legal aid.
