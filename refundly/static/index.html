BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few fair
questions.

Is Refundly a law firm?＋

No. Refundly is an informational tool and document generator. Assessments are preliminary and depend on your answers. A lawyer or local legal-aid service can help with advice specific to your situation.

What should I have ready?＋

Your deposit amount, move-in and move-out dates, any refund, and the landlord’s list of deductions. Photos, receipts, and your lease can help you evaluate the explanation. You don’t need to upload documents for this demo.

How much does it cost?＋

The example price is $19 per document pack. Checkout is simulated in this local demo: no payment details are collected and no money is charged. The download includes the letter, an evidence checklist, and sending instructions.

Will you contact my landlord?＋

No. You can edit and download your letter, then send it yourself. Email delivery and automatic email reminders are not connected. You can download a calendar reminder for your own follow-up.

Does this guarantee I’ll get my money back?＋

No. Evidence, your lease, applicable law, and the facts of the dispute determine the outcome. Refundly helps you understand and question deductions; it cannot decide a legal claim.
